# Use cases

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

- Your [AI model is connected](/en/getting-started/setup-ai).
- Copy any sentence below and swap in your own details. Where a file is needed, click **＋** to the left of the message box and choose **Files and folders** first.
- Rows marked "Digital Human" have the AI work on its own schedule, or are said in a Digital Human's own chat; see the [Digital Humans overview](/en/digital-humans/overview).

## Office admin {#office}

| What you want | You could say | More |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting minutes | Turn the attached meeting notes into minutes with decisions and action items, each with an owner, as a Word document | [Office documents](/en/features/office-documents#make-a-document) |
| A notice | Write a notice about next Wednesday afternoon's fire drill, formal tone, under 200 words | [Your first task](/en/getting-started/first-chat) |
| A weekly reminder (Digital Human) | In a Digital Human's chat: Remind me every Friday at 4 pm to hand in my weekly report | [Reminders](/en/digital-humans/reminders) |

## Marketing and operations {#marketing}

| What you want | You could say | More |
|---|---|---|
| Compare competitors | Open these three competitors' websites, collect their prices and plans, and make an Excel comparison table | [AI Browser](/en/features/ai-browser) |
| A daily industry brief (Digital Human) | Create a Digital Human that, every weekday at 9 am, sums up the previous day's industry news in 5 points | [Create a Digital Human](/en/digital-humans/create) |
| An article with a reviewer | Put together a small team: one researcher, one writer and one reviewer, and write a launch post for our new product | [Digital Human teams](/en/digital-humans/collaboration) |

## Saying it well {#tips}

- Say what result you want: the format, the length, who it's for.
- Provide the material: attach files, or say which folder they're in.
- Say when it should check with you, for example "ask me first if anything is unclear".
- One job at a time; for a big job, ask for a team and let several Digital Humans split it.

# Glossary

Terms you meet in these docs and in the app, grouped by topic. Each one has a short definition, the labels the app shows, and the page that explains it.

## Workspace {#workspace}

A folder plus its conversations, memory and resources. Use one per project.

- Chinese: 工作区
- In the app: "Workspace", "Halo Workspace", "New Space", "Manage workspaces"
- More: [Workspaces](/en/features/spaces)

## Halo Workspace {#halo-workspace}

The built-in workspace Halo opens in; good for one-off work.

- Chinese: Halo 工作区
- In the app: "Halo Workspace"
- More: [Workspaces](/en/features/spaces)

## Conversation {#conversation}

One chat thread with Halo inside a workspace.

- Chinese: 会话
- In the app: "Conversation", "Conversations", "New"
- More: [Conversations](/en/features/conversations)

## Digital Human {#digital-human}

An AI member that works on its own — on a schedule, on events or when messaged in IM — with its own memory and settings.

- Chinese: 数字人
- In the app: "Digital Human", "Digital Humans", "My Digital Humans"
- More: [Digital Humans overview](/en/digital-humans/overview)

## Team {#team}

Several Digital Humans led by a lead that split a job, hand work over and deliver results; temporary or saved.

- Chinese: 团队
- In the app: "Teams", "Keep this team"
- More: [Digital Human teams](/en/digital-humans/collaboration)

## Temporary collaboration {#temporary-collaboration}

A team the AI assembles for the task at hand; keep it if you want to reuse it.

- Chinese: 临时协作
- In the app: "Temporary collaboration"
- More: [Digital Human teams](/en/digital-humans/collaboration)

## Lead {#lead}

The team member that plans, assigns work and asks you when the team cannot decide.

- Chinese: 负责人
- In the app: "Lead"
- More: [Digital Human teams](/en/digital-humans/collaboration)

## Team across computers {#cross-computer-team}

A team with members on colleagues' computers, joined by an invite link. Members on the same network connect straight to the host computer (with Remote Access on); across networks they go through a Federation Gateway you run yourselves.

- Chinese: 跨电脑团队
- In the app: "Invite", "Invite link", "Join a team"
- More: [Teams across computers](/en/digital-humans/cross-computer-teams)

## Federation Gateway {#federation-gateway}

A relay you deploy yourself so computers on different networks can join one team; it passes messages between them.

- Chinese: 联邦网关
- In the app: "Federation Gateway"
- More: [Deploy the Federation Gateway](/en/advanced/federation-gateway)

## Capabilities {#capabilities}

Per-conversation switches in the composer ＋. The AI can ask you to turn one on but cannot turn it on itself.

- Chinese: 能力
- In the app: "Capabilities", "Web Control", "AI Terminal", "Text Recognition", "Operate Halo", "Team Collaboration"
- More: [Capabilities](/en/features/capabilities)

## Capability library {#capability-library}

Where installed skills and MCP servers are managed.

- Chinese: 能力库
- In the app: "Capability library"
- More: [Skills](/en/features/skills)

## Skill {#skill}

A reusable instruction package (SKILL.md) the AI uses when relevant, or that you call with /.

- Chinese: 技能
- In the app: "Skills"
- More: [Skills](/en/features/skills)

## MCP server {#mcp-server}

An external tool server connected through the Model Context Protocol.

- Chinese: MCP 服务器
- In the app: "MCP"
- More: [MCP servers](/en/features/mcp)

## Knowledge base {#knowledge-base}

Your documents, extracted on your computer into searchable text; the AI reads them and lists its sources.

- Chinese: 知识库
- In the app: "Knowledge Base", "Knowledge", "Sources"
- More: [Knowledge Base](/en/features/knowledge-base)

## Memory {#memory}

What Halo keeps between conversations: conversations in a workspace share workspace memory; each Digital Human has its own (current state, history, topics).

- Chinese: 记忆
- In the app: "Memory", "Workspace memory", "Consolidate now", "Automatic consolidation", "Use workspace memory", "Clear Memory"
- More: [Memory](/en/features/memory)

## Topic {#topic}

One page of memory about one subject, written and tidied by the AI.

- Chinese: 主题
- More: [Memory](/en/features/memory)

## Task panel {#task-panel}

One list of running work and questions waiting for you, across all workspaces.

- Chinese: 任务面板
- In the app: "Tasks", "Needs you", "Running", "Pinned"
- More: [The task panel](/en/features/tasks)

## Changes view {#changes}

See, stage and commit Git changes in the workspace, and have the AI review them.

- Chinese: 变更
- In the app: "Changes", "View changes", "Quick review", "Team review"
- More: [Changes, code review and Git](/en/features/code-review)

## Canvas {#canvas}

The tabbed viewer on the right for files, the browser, the terminal, the team view and changes.

- Chinese: 画布
- In the app: "Open in canvas"
- More: [Files and the canvas](/en/features/files-and-canvas)

## Workspace resources {#workspace-resources}

The right rail with Files, Digital Humans, Skill and MCP tabs.

- Chinese: 工作区资源
- In the app: "Open workspace resources"
- More: [Files and the canvas](/en/features/files-and-canvas)

## AI Browser {#ai-browser}

A built-in browser the AI operates with your signed-in sessions; you can watch and take over.

- Chinese: AI 浏览器
- In the app: "Open browser", "Web Control"
- More: [AI Browser](/en/features/ai-browser)

## AI Terminal {#ai-terminal}

A persistent terminal you and the AI share.

- Chinese: AI 终端
- In the app: "AI Terminal", "Open terminal"
- More: [AI Terminal](/en/features/ai-terminal)

## Store {#store}

Browse and install Digital Humans and skills; Settings › App Store manages the sources.

- Chinese: 商店
- In the app: "Store", "App Store", "Publish to Store"
- More: [Store](/en/digital-humans/store)

## Message channels {#message-channels}

Connections to IM and email: two-way bots bound to one Digital Human, and one-way notification channels.

- Chinese: 消息通道
- In the app: "Message Channels", "WeCom Intelligent Bot", "Feishu Bot", "WeChat Bot", "Email", "Webhook"
- More: [Message channels overview](/en/digital-humans/channels)

## Bot {#bot}

The identity on the IM side (WeCom, Feishu, WeChat) that relays messages to one Digital Human.

- Chinese: 机器人
- More: [Message channels overview](/en/digital-humans/channels)

## Reachable Contacts {#reachable-contacts}

The chats a Digital Human may send messages to on its own.

- Chinese: 可联系的联系人
- In the app: "Reachable Contacts", "Add from existing chats", "Sent proactively"
- More: [Notifications and proactive push](/en/digital-humans/notifications)

## Permission Control {#permission-control}

Decides what people messaging a bot can make its Digital Human do: the owner has full access, guests only the allowed tools.

- Chinese: 权限控制
- In the app: "Permission Control", "Guest Permissions", "Guest Allowed Tools", "Guest Access"
- More: [Owner and guest permissions](/en/digital-humans/permissions)

## Owner / guest {#owner-guest}

The owner is you, as the bot knows you: the first person to message the bot directly becomes its owner. Guests are everyone else who messages the bot.

- Chinese: 主人 / 访客
- More: [Owner and guest permissions](/en/digital-humans/permissions)

## Reminder {#reminder}

A one-off or recurring reminder you set by asking a Digital Human in its chat.

- Chinese: 提醒
- In the app: "Reminders", "Cancel this reminder"
- More: [Reminders](/en/digital-humans/reminders)

## Schedule {#schedule}

When a Digital Human runs by itself.

- Chinese: 计划任务
- In the app: "Schedule", "Run now", "Only on mainland China working days"
- More: [Schedules and triggers](/en/digital-humans/triggers)

## Trigger {#trigger}

What starts a run: a schedule, an event or a message.

- Chinese: 触发器
- In the app: "Trigger"
- More: [Schedules and triggers](/en/digital-humans/triggers)

## Run {#run}

One execution of a Digital Human's job, with a record you can open.

- Chinese: 运行
- In the app: "Run", "Not started"
- More: [Runs and management](/en/digital-humans/manage)

## Capabilities and settings {#dh-settings}

A Digital Human's settings: model, tools and resources, memory, notifications and more.

- Chinese: 能力与设置
- In the app: "Capabilities and settings", "Tools & Resources"
- More: [Capabilities and settings](/en/digital-humans/capabilities)

## Model / AI source / provider {#model}

An AI source is one account or key; each offers models; the model is chosen per conversation.

- Chinese: 模型 / AI 来源 / 提供商
- In the app: "AI Model", "Configure AI Source", "Manage AI Provider", "Switch model"
- More: [Models and accounts](/en/features/models)

## Deep Thinking {#deep-thinking}

How much the model reasons before answering, from Off to Max, set per conversation.

- Chinese: 深度思考
- In the app: "Deep Thinking"
- More: [Models and accounts](/en/features/models)

## Remaining quota {#quota}

The allowance left, shown only when the provider reports it.

- Chinese: 剩余配额
- In the app: "Remaining quota"
- More: [Models and accounts](/en/features/models)

## Agent engine {#agent-engine}

The runtime that drives the AI's work: Claude Code SDK by default, plus Halo SDK, Codex SDK and DeepSeek dsh (the last three are experimental).

- Chinese: Agent 引擎
- In the app: "AI Agent Engine"
- More: [Agent engines](/en/features/agent-engines)

## Remote Access {#remote-access}

Open your Halo from a phone or another device, on your network or over the internet; also the door to the HTTP API.

- Chinese: 远程访问
- In the app: "Remote Access"
- More: [Remote Access](/en/features/remote-access)

## Operate Halo {#operate-halo}

A capability that lets the AI change settings and manage workspaces and Digital Humans through Halo's own API.

- Chinese: 操作 Halo
- In the app: "Operate Halo"
- More: [Let the AI operate Halo](/en/features/operate-halo)

## Reference a conversation {#reference-conversation}

Let the AI read another conversation in the workspace, or message it directly.

- Chinese: 引用会话
- In the app: "Reference a conversation", "Chat with a digital human"
- More: [Conversations that work together](/en/features/cross-conversation)

## Goal {#goal}

An objective plus "done when" criteria set for a conversation; it survives context compaction.

- Chinese: 目标
- In the app: "Goal"
- More: [Conversations](/en/features/conversations)

## Processing Notice {#processing-notice}

The "working on it" message a bot sends in IM when a reply takes a while; can be turned off.

- Chinese: 处理中提示
- In the app: "Processing Notice"
- More: [WeCom bot: advanced](/en/digital-humans/wecom-bot-features)

## Digital Human package {#package}

A shareable Digital Human file: .dhpkg, .zip, .yaml, or a folder with spec.yaml.

- Chinese: 数字人包
- More: [Runs and management](/en/digital-humans/manage)

## Halo Server {#halo-server}

Headless Halo run as a service, with the same UI in a browser.

- Chinese: Halo Server
- More: [Halo Server quickstart](/en/server/)

## Developer Mode {#developer-mode}

Writes more detailed diagnostic logs.

- Chinese: 开发者模式
- In the app: "Developer Mode"
- More: [Logs and diagnostics](/en/troubleshooting/diagnostics)
